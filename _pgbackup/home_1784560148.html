About the magazine

About LEXEME

Lexeme is inspired by the linguistic term for a meaningful unit of language. The name reflects the magazine’s linguistic, educational, and cultural identity.

It is a student-run quarterly publication created by the English Language Association at the Faculty of Educational Sciences, Farhangian University of Bushehr. The magazine gives students a space to share ideas, experiences and creativity through education, language, literature, culture, art and student life.
